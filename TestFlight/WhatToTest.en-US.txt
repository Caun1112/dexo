What to Test

- React to a post and confirm the feedback feels quick and clear. Tap again to remove the reaction and confirm it stays quiet.
- Open an image in a post, then pull down to close it and confirm the gesture feels natural.
- Tap an in-forum topic link to a specific post from within a post, and confirm the topic opens directly at that post.
- Check solution preview expansion and acceptance in a Q&A topic, then switch themes and confirm the solution card and active search filters follow the accent color.
