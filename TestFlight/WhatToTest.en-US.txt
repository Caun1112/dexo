What to Test

- Open the Me tab several times and confirm cached profile information appears immediately without repeated loading states.
- Pull to refresh the Me tab and confirm the latest profile data is fetched while the existing profile remains visible during refresh.
- Verify the profile card, avatar, bio, join date, and four statistics display correctly in both light and dark themes.
- Open a topic with long text, switch the system between light and dark mode, and confirm all body text and code areas update their colors immediately.
- Scroll through a topic before switching appearance and confirm the reading position is preserved and newly loaded posts use the current theme.
- Try adding a Cloudflare-protected linux.do forum. Confirm the app shows a challenge prompt with an Open Challenge action.
- Complete the challenge, return to Add Forum, and tap Add Forum again to verify the forum is added successfully.
- Cancel the challenge or leave it incomplete and confirm the forum is not added.
- Verify existing linux.do challenge prompts from forum features still open the same challenge page.
